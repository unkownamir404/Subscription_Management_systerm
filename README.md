# Subscription_Management_systerm
Subscription Management System – Detailed App Features
1. User Authentication & Account Management
- User registration with unique email address
- Secure login and logout
- Password validation (uppercase, lowercase, special character, minimum length)
- Forgot password and email-based password reset
- Role-based access control (Admin, Staff, Customer)
- Admin-only staff account creation
2. Product Management
- Add, edit, delete and view products
- Product name, type, sales price, cost price and description
- Product variant management (brand, model, etc.)
- Automatic price adjustment based on selected variants
- Product activation and deactivation
3. Subscription Plan Management
- Create and manage subscription plans
- Set plan price and billing frequency
- Billing cycles: Daily, Weekly, Monthly and Yearly
- Set minimum subscription quantity
- Define plan start and end dates
- Configure automatic subscription closure
- Allow subscription pause, cancellation and renewal
4. Quotation Management
- Create reusable quotation templates
- Add products and subscription plans to quotations
- Set quotation validity period
- Generate quotations for customers
- Convert quotations into subscriptions
- Track quotation status (Draft, Quotation, Confirmed)
5. Subscription Management
- Create unique subscription numbers
- Assign customers and subscription plans
- Set start date, expiry date and payment terms
- Add multiple products to a subscription
- Manage quantity, unit price, taxes and total amount
- Track subscription lifecycle (Draft, Quotation, Confirmed, Active, Closed)
- Renew, pause or cancel subscriptions
6. Automated Billing & Invoice Management
- Automatically generate invoices on billing dates
- Generate invoices directly from subscriptions
- Automatically populate customer and product details
- Calculate quantity, taxes and invoice totals
- Prevent duplicate or missed invoices
- Confirm, cancel, send and print invoices
- Track invoice status (Draft, Confirmed, Paid)
7. Tax Management
- Admin-configurable tax rules
- Create and manage different tax rates
- Apply taxes to products and subscription plans
- Automatic tax calculation during invoicing
- Display tax breakdown in invoices
8. Discount & Coupon Management
- Admin-only discount rule creation
- Fixed amount and percentage-based discounts
- Set minimum purchase amount or quantity
- Configure discount start and expiry dates
- Set maximum discount usage limits
- Apply discounts to selected products or entire subscriptions
- Automatically apply eligible discounts to invoices
- Automatically expire discounts when limits or dates are reached
9. Payment Management
- Record customer payments against invoices
- Support multiple payment methods
- Store payment amount, date, method and notes
- Link payments to the correct invoice
- Track full and outstanding payment amounts
- Maintain payment history
- Identify unpaid and overdue invoices
10. Customer Management & Self-Service Portal
- View and manage customer profiles
- Access individual customer subscription details
- View subscription status and renewal dates
- View, download and print invoices
- Check payment history and outstanding balances
- Manage subscriptions without contacting staff
- Restrict access to customers' own data
11. Dashboard & Analytics
- Overview of total and active subscriptions
- Total recurring revenue
- Total revenue collected
- Pending and outstanding payments
- Overdue invoice summary
- Subscription renewal overview
- Revenue and payment statistics
- Quick access to important billing information
12. Reports & Financial Tracking
- Generate revenue reports
- Subscription status reports
- Invoice reports
- Payment collection reports
- Outstanding balance reports
- Overdue invoice reports
- Filter reports by date, customer and status
- View monthly, quarterly and yearly financial summaries
13. Notifications & Reminders
- Email confirmation after registration
- Password reset emails
- Invoice generation notifications
- Invoice due-date reminders
- Payment confirmation notifications
- Subscription renewal reminders
- Subscription expiry notifications
- Overdue payment alerts
14. Search, Filtering & Performance
- Search customers, products and subscriptions
- Filter invoices by status and date
- Filter subscriptions by plan and status
- Sort records by date, amount and customer
- Handle thousands of subscriptions efficiently
- Ensure pages load within two seconds
